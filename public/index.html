A MINUTE TO LEARN

Don’t be the last
holding cards.

  1. Everyone starts with six cards. Ranks run from 6 up to Ace. The face-up card sets the trump suit; it is drawn last.
  2. The lowest trump starts. Play any opening card. The defender beats it with a higher card of the same suit, or a trump. Only a higher trump beats a trump.
  3. After each defence, attackers may add a card matching any rank on the table. The lead attacker gets first choice, then the others clockwise. Tap Pass to let the next attacker decide.
  4. The defender can Take cards. Attackers can still add matching cards before passing. There are at most six attacks, or the defender’s starting hand size if smaller.
  5. When everyone passes, a defended table is discarded. If taking, the defender collects the whole table and skips their next attack.
  6. Draw back to six: lead attacker first, other attackers clockwise, defender last. Once the deck is empty, empty hands are out. The last player with cards is the Durak.

This table plays individual, one-card-at-a-time throw-in Durak. No transfers, teams, or trump-six exchange. Without a legal attack you pass automatically; without a legal defence you take automatically. Leaving removes your hand from play. If the defender leaves, the current table is discarded and a new bout starts. With two or more players the game continues; otherwise the lobby waits for a new player. A lost connection still keeps your seat for reconnection.
